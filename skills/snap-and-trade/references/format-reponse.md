# Output formats

Use the user's language. Default is **short**, about 120–150 words excluding
the table. No long reasoning, full indicator inventory or module advertising.
Give observed facts, decision, levels, trigger and material limitations.

## Short card (default)

**NO TRADE NOW — conditional SHORT · CAC 40 · 5m / 1h**

Score: **2/3 available**, confidence medium (descriptive, not win probability).
Unavailable: volume, momentum. Latest price: **8372**, axis label; header
8394 and panel 8361 differ. [Illustrative example, not a current signal.]

| Level | Price | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry | 8372 | Only after the stated closed rejection |
| Stop | 8390 | Beyond invalidating swing; distance 18, mean candle 10, floor 15 |
| TP1 / TP2 | 8340 / 8320 | Gross R:R 1.78 / 2.89; costs unknown |

**Why wait:** the closed trigger is not confirmed. Ask for fresh images once
it closes. Sizing omitted until capital and exact instrument are confirmed.

“Explain” for detail; optionally offer to log the scenario.

Never copy these example values into a user's analysis. The score, prices,
mean range and context must be observed and checked by the scripts.

## Closed market card

**NO TRADE NOW — OPENING PLAN · instrument · execution / context UT**

Show the same compact levels plus:
- Against the idea at/beyond SL at opening → cancel.
- Through TP1 in the intended direction → cancel, do not chase.
- Between SL and TP1 → refresh both charts, await the closed trigger, rerun
  stop floor and net R:R at the actual entry. No old limit order is authorized.
- Session/date/timezone to verify; expiry at the next intended session.

If higher timeframe or readable price/time is missing, lead with that single
blocking request. Do not hide it under a generic low-confidence label.

## Detailed mode (only when asked to explain)

Expand with: exact instrument/session; price-source discrepancy; execution and
higher structure; observed levels; selected setup; available-positive,
available-negative and unavailable criteria; candle-range sample; script
commands and results; costs/quantity assumptions; trigger, expiry and opening
conditions. Keep observations separate from estimates and conditions.

A valid arithmetic plan is not confirmation of the chart interpretation.
Never call a pending trigger an active LONG/SHORT signal. Use text labels in
addition to emoji so color is not required for understanding.
